Stumble

Guess the word, then fall down a rabbit hole.

Start typing


How to play

  1. Each round hides one word. The boxes show how many letters it has, and the green label gives its category, such as person or place.
  2. Type a guess and press Enter. Backspace deletes a letter. On a phone, use the keys under the board. Guesses don’t have to be real words.
  3. After each guess, the boxes change colour:
    • ARight letter, right spot
    • BIn the word, wrong spot
    • CNot in the word
  4. You get one more guess than the word has letters. Stuck? Press Show hint.
  5. Solve it, or run out of guesses, to read the story behind the word. Then press Next word. Answers can be names, places or shortened titles.
